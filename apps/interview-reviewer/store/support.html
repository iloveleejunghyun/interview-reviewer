Record your job interview. Get question-by-question feedback.

Need help? Email ilovejunghyun@yahoo.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Getting a good recording

  • Online interviews: use your laptop or tablet's speaker, not headphones, and put your phone next to it. The phone can only record what it can hear.
  • In-person interviews: place the phone on the table between you and the interviewer.
  • You can lock the screen while recording. Recording continues until you tap Stop.
  • Always ask the interviewer for permission before recording.

Common questions

How long does the review take?

About a minute for most interviews, and up to 2 minutes for an hour-long one. If you leave the app while it's processing, it continues automatically when you come back.

It says there wasn't enough speech to review.

The recording didn't pick up enough of the conversation. This usually means the interview used headphones or the phone was too far from the speaker. Record at least a minute of conversation, with the phone close to the sound.

The review says a question was "reconstructed".

Sometimes a question isn't captured clearly in the recording. When your answer makes it obvious what was asked, the review reconstructs the question and labels it, so you know it's an inference.

What are the [brackets] in the stronger answers?

The stronger answer only uses what you actually said. Where a great answer needs details only you know, like a result or a number, it leaves a [placeholder] for you to fill in, instead of inventing achievements you'd have to defend in an interview.

Transcription or review failed.

Check your internet connection, then open the interview and tap Retry. If it keeps failing, email us with the error message shown.

Where is my data?

Recordings, transcripts, and reviews are stored on your iPhone. To process them, audio is sent to Volcengine and the transcript to Baidu AI Cloud Qianfan, as shown on the consent screen. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my interviews?

Deleting the app removes all recordings, transcripts, and reviews stored on your device.
